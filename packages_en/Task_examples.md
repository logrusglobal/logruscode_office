# Example tasks for OpenCode (OfficeCLI + browser agent)

What you can do in plain words in the chat — ready-made phrases for the first demo to an employee and for everyday work. Just copy a phrase into the chat and substitute your own data.

## Excel (OfficeCLI)

- "Create a contacts table: columns — company, contact person, phone, e-mail, website. Fill it with the data I send you."
- "Sum up column B in budget.xlsx and add a 'Total' row."
- "Merge the data from january.xlsx, february.xlsx and march.xlsx into one summary table with a 'Month' column."
- "Compare supplier_price.xlsx with our current price_list.xlsx and mark the rows where the price has changed."
- "Fill in the report_template.xlsx template with data: project, languages, volume, deadlines — and calculate the total cost at a rate of 0.25 per word."
- "Read project_plan.xlsx and make a list of the tasks whose deadline falls within this week."
- "Translate all the service names in price_list.xlsx into English and write them into the second column."

## Word (OfficeCLI)

- "Create a letter to a client: subject — order confirmation, address them by name, body text and a table with the order details."
- "Lay out a project report following this outline: goals, work completed, statistics, issues, conclusions. Make the title and subheadings stand out."
- "Read contract.docx and write out all the dates and amounts into a table: contract clause, obligation, deadline, amount."
- "Fix the typos and the style in client_letter.docx, save it under a new name."
- "Translate article.docx into English and save the translation next to the original."
- "Turn the report text into a half-page executive summary and lay it out with a heading."

## Browser (collecting information from websites)

- "Collect the contacts from https://… : company name, phone, e-mail, address — and save them to Excel."
- "Find the page with the partnership terms on https://… and write out the main points."
- "Download the price list from https://… and consolidate the prices into an Excel table."
- "Check our website https://… : are there any errors on the page, open the console and show the error messages."
- "Find a list of translation companies in Moscow online with their phone numbers and websites, save it to Excel."
- "Open our internal system, find project 1234 and write out its status and deadlines."

## "Browser + OfficeCLI" combined (end-to-end tasks)

- "Collect the contacts from https://… and https://… into Excel, then create a proposal letter in Word for each company."
- "Find the product documentation on the customer's website, download it and put together a short summary in Word."
- "Collect the service lists and prices from three competitors' websites and consolidate them into a comparison table in Excel."

## How to show it to an employee at the first meeting (5 minutes)

1. Open OpenCode, show that the agent is "office" and the model is DeepSeek.
2. Task 1 (Word): "Create a letter to a client with a details table" — then open the resulting file in Word.
3. Task 2 (Excel): "Sum up the values in my file" — and show the result.
4. Task 3 (browser, if installed): launch the "Browser for OpenCode" shortcut and ask "Collect the contacts from the website …" — show how Chrome opens and works on its own.
5. Emphasise: tasks are written in plain words, nothing needs to be configured.
